    feat(trimul): residual+dropout for the bidirectional path (training fused + inference)
    
    Extend the residual + row-broadcast dropout fusion to BidirectionalTriangleMultiplication.
    Its training back-half (BidirBackHalf) uses the same gate_elem kernels as the single-dir v6,
    so the fusion is identical.
    
    - bidir_training BidirBackHalf: takes `pair` (residual) + `dropscale`; fwd fuses them into the
      gate (y = pair + dropscale ⊙ trimul(pair)), bwd scales the incoming grad by dropscale and
      returns d_residual = gy. Threaded through bidir_forward + BidirV6TriMul.forward.
    - module: `forward(pair, mask, add_residual, dropout_p)` + `_make_drop_row_scale`; training fuses
      via _forward_cute_train (sm90) / explicit on sm100; inference & non-cute backends apply the
      residual (+ dropout, training) via `_r`.
    - pairformer: bidirectional update now `tri_multi(pair, mask, add_residual=True, dropout_p=...)`.
    
    H100: inference residual cos 0.99999, training p=0 residual dx grad cos 1.0, dropout deterministic
    under seed and applied. sm90 (triton gate_elem) fuses; sm100 bidir applies explicitly.
